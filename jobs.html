Strategy Long View Attendance Optional Remote / Deep
Senior Strategy Consultant
Department: Long View Strategy  |  Reports To: Nessie (Head of Long View Strategy)  |  Location: Calendar-Adjacent / Undisclosed

Tiny Riot Inc.'s Long View Strategy division is accepting one (1) consultant to support Nessie's ongoing strategic operations. This role requires exceptional patience, a tolerance for deep silences, and the ability to understand what is meant rather than what is said. Nessie will surface infrequently. When she does, she will say something. It will be important. The rest of the time, you will be expected to hold the line. The line is ancient. It has held before.

  • Long view thinking — minimum 200 years of perspective strongly preferred; exceptions considered case by case
  • Comfortable with extended periods of silence (this is most of the role)
  • Calendar availability required; attendance is optional and at Nessie's discretion
  • Ability to surface briefly, contribute one decisive point, and return — this is the model
  • No preference for credit, recognition, or follow-up discussion
  • Must not interpret strategic ambiguity as an invitation to fill the silence
What We Offer
  • Decisions respected regardless of attendance record
  • Flexible workspace arrangements — aquatic, remote, and calendar-adjacent options available
  • Nobody will ask what you meant, or when you will surface again
  • HR will not schedule wellness check-ins for this role
  • Mentorship from an entity who has watched the long arc of many things and has an opinion on all of them
How to Apply: If Nessie has noticed you, the process has already begun. If you are unsure whether she has noticed you, she has.
